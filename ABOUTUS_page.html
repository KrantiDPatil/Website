About Government Polytechnic, Pen


A legacy of acadamic excellence, innovation, and student success since 1990.


- Know Us Better -

A Legacy of excellence,
since 1990


Government Polytechnic College Pen, established in 1990, is a government institute affiliated with the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education, Mumbai. The college offers Post SSC Diploma programs in Engineering and has a total intake of 260 students. It is governed by the Hon. Director, Directorate of Technical Education, Mumbai. The institute has a vision to develop competent professionals for industrial and social growth and offers various facilities to support students' academic and personal needs.

Government Polytechnic Pen admissions are offered to various specialisation courses at diploma level. These specialisation courses are Diploma in Civil Engineering, Diploma in Mechanical Engineering, Diploma in Instrumentation and Control Engineering and other specialised courses. Government Polytechnic Pen is affiliated to Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education, Mumbai. Government Polytechnic Pen has a dedicated Training and Placement Cell.

The highest package offered for diploma placed students in 2022-23 is Rs 3.60 LPA. Some of the top recruiters that visited Government Polytechnic Pen include Mahindra, Bharat Petroleum, Bajaj, Larsen & Toubro and many more. Government Polytechnic Pen provides various facilities to the students which include auditorium, guest room, medica facility, cafeteria, alumni associations, sports facility and some other facilities.
