- About Chemical Engineering -


The Chemical Engineering Department at Government Polytechnic Pen is committed to providing quality technical education and practical training in the field of chemical engineering. The department follows the curriculum prescribed by the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), Mumbai, and focuses on developing a strong foundation in chemical process technology, process equipment, industrial chemistry, heat and mass transfer, fluid mechanics, and environmental engineering. Students gain practical knowledge through laboratory experiments, industrial visits, and project-based learning, enabling them to understand modern chemical industries and manufacturing processes.

The department is equipped with well-maintained laboratories and modern equipment that provide hands-on experience in chemical analysis, process operations, and industrial safety practices. Experienced faculty members guide students through technical seminars, workshops, laboratory sessions, and industrial training programs to enhance their technical knowledge and problem-solving abilities. Students are encouraged to participate in technical events, research-oriented activities, and skill development programs to keep pace with advancements in chemical engineering and process industries.

The Chemical Engineering Department works closely with the Training and Placement Cell to prepare students for careers in the chemical and process industries. Students are encouraged to undertake internships, industrial training, and certification programs to improve their practical exposure and employability. Graduates of the department have career opportunities as process engineers, production engineers, quality control engineers, plant operators, safety officers, and laboratory analysts in chemical, pharmaceutical, petrochemical, fertilizer, food processing, and other manufacturing industries.
