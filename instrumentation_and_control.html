- About Instumentation and Control -


The Instrumentation and Control Engineering Department at Government Polytechnic Pen is dedicated to developing skilled diploma engineers in the field of industrial automation, measurement, and control systems. The department follows the curriculum prescribed by the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), Mumbai, with a strong emphasis on both theoretical concepts and practical applications. Students gain knowledge in instrumentation systems, industrial sensors, process control, programmable logic controllers (PLC), microcontrollers, electronic measurements, and industrial automation through well-equipped laboratories and project-based learning.

The department provides modern laboratory facilities equipped with advanced instruments, process control trainers, PLC kits, calibration equipment, and measurement devices to enhance practical learning. Experienced faculty members guide students through laboratory experiments, technical seminars, industrial visits, workshops, and mini projects that help develop analytical thinking, technical skills, and problem-solving abilities. Students are encouraged to participate in technical events and skill development programs to keep pace with advancements in automation and control technologies.
The Instrumentation and Control Engineering Department actively supports students through the Training and Placement Cell by organizing industrial training, internships, and career guidance programs. Students are encouraged to earn industry-relevant certifications and gain practical exposure to modern automation technologies. Graduates of the department have career opportunities as instrumentation engineers, automation engineers, PLC programmers, process control technicians, maintenance engineers, calibration engineers, and quality control professionals in manufacturing industries, power plants, oil and gas, chemical industries, and other industrial sectors.
