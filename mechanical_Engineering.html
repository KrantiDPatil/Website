- About Mechanical Engineering -

The Mechanical Engineering Department at Government Polytechnic Pen is committed to providing quality technical education and practical training in the field of mechanical engineering. The department follows the curriculum prescribed by the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), Mumbai, and focuses on building strong fundamentals in engineering mechanics, manufacturing processes, thermal engineering, machine design, CAD/CAM, and industrial maintenance. Students gain practical knowledge through laboratory work, workshop practice, and project-based learning that prepares them for industrial applications.

The department is equipped with well-maintained laboratories, machine shops, workshops, and modern equipment to enhance students' technical skills and hands-on experience. Experienced faculty members guide students through technical seminars, industrial visits, workshops, and skill development programs that encourage innovation, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities. Students are also motivated to participate in technical competitions and co-curricular activities to strengthen their overall engineering knowledge.

The Mechanical Engineering Department works closely with the Training and Placement Cell to prepare students for successful careers in the engineering industry. Students are encouraged to undertake internships, industrial training, and certification programs to improve their practical exposure and employability. Graduates of the department have opportunities to work as production engineers, maintenance engineers, design engineers, quality control engineers, CAD technicians, and manufacturing professionals in various public and private sector industries.
