- About Computer Technology -


The Computer Technology Department at Government Polytechnic Pen is dedicated to providing quality technical education in the field of computer science and information technology. The department follows the curriculum prescribed by the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), Mumbai, with a balanced focus on theoretical knowledge and practical learning. Students gain strong fundamentals in programming, database management, computer networks, operating systems, web development, software engineering, and emerging technologies through well-structured academic and laboratory sessions.

The department is equipped with modern computer laboratories, high-speed internet connectivity, and the latest software tools to support effective learning and skill development. Experienced faculty members encourage students to participate in technical seminars, workshops, coding competitions, industrial visits, and project-based learning activities. The department also promotes innovation, teamwork, and problem-solving skills through various academic and extracurricular initiatives.

The Computer Technology Department has an active Training and Placement Cell that assists students in developing technical, communication, and interview skills. Students are encouraged to pursue internships, industry projects, and certification courses to improve their employability. Graduates of the department have opportunities to build successful careers as software developers, web developers, system administrators, database administrators, network engineers, and IT support professionals in leading industries and organizations.
