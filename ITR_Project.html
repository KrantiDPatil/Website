Government Polytechnic Pen is committed to providing quality technical education and developing skilled professionals for the future. Our institution offers various diploma programs, modern facilities, experienced faculty, and a student-friendly learning environment.


Principal's Message




Welcome to Government Polytechnic Pen! In an era of rapid technological advancement and global connectivity, the nature of learning is constantly evolving. Our goal at Government Polytechnic Pen is to foster a culture of curiosity, critical thinking, and lifelong learning. We do not just impart knowledge; we equip our students with the skills required to navigate and thrive in an increasingly competitive world. We offer an array of programs designed to meet international standards, paired with research opportunities and community outreach that shape well-rounded individuals. Our vision is to be a quality-driven center of excellence, inspiring innovation and ethical leadership. Whether you are a prospective student, a parent, or an active member of our community, I encourage you to partner with us on this exciting journey. Let us work hand in hand to create a brighter, more sustainable future. At Government Polytechnic Pen, we believe that education is the foundation of progress and the key to unlocking every student's true potential. Together, let us build a future driven by knowledge, innovation, integrity, and a commitment to serving society.

Courses we offer

Computer Technology

Develop skills in programming,
web development, databases, networking, and software engineering for careers in the IT industry.


Mechanical Engineering


Learn machine design, manufacturing processes, thermal engineering, CAD/CAM, and industrial maintenance.


Instrumentation and Control Engineering


Focus on industrial automation, sensors, PLCs, process instrumentation, and control systems used in modern industries.

Chemical Engineering

Gain knowledge of chemical
processes, plant operations, process control, safety, and industrial production techniques.


Civil Engineering

Study construction technology, surveying, structural design, transportation engineering, and sustainable infrastructure.

    Government Polytechnic College Pen, established in
