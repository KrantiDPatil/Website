- About Civil Engineering -

The Civil Engineering Department at Government Polytechnic Pen is dedicated to providing quality technical education and practical training in the field of civil engineering. The department follows the curriculum prescribed by the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), Mumbai, with a strong focus on both theoretical knowledge and practical applications. Students gain a solid understanding of structural engineering, surveying, construction technology, transportation engineering, geotechnical engineering, and environmental engineering through classroom learning, laboratory sessions, and fieldwork.

The department is equipped with well-maintained laboratories for surveying, concrete technology, geotechnical engineering, highway engineering, and material testing, enabling students to develop practical skills and technical expertise. Experienced faculty members guide students through technical seminars, workshops, industrial visits, and project-based learning activities that encourage innovation, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities. Students are also motivated to participate in technical competitions and skill development programs to enhance their professional knowledge.

The Civil Engineering Department actively supports students through the Training and Placement Cell by organizing industrial training, internships, and career guidance programs. Students are encouraged to gain practical exposure through site visits and industry interactions to improve their employability. Graduates of the department have excellent career opportunities as site engineers, structural engineers, surveyors, quality control engineers, construction supervisors, project coordinators, and government engineers in the construction, infrastructure, transportation, and public works sectors.
